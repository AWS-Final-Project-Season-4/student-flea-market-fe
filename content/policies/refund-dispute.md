# Return, Refund & Dispute Resolution Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026

## 1. Scope & Nature of Transactions
Student Flea Market provides a peer-to-peer (P2P) platform designed for university students to exchange, buy, and sell second-hand or academic items. Because goods are predominantly pre-owned and sold by individual student sellers, standard commercial return terms may differ based on transaction types:
- **On-Campus In-Person Transactions (Meetups)**.
- **Delivery / Shipping Transactions**.

## 2. In-Person Transactions (On-Campus Handover)
For safety and verification, the Platform strongly encourages direct campus meetups.
- **Inspection Responsibility**: The Buyer has the right and duty to inspect the item physically (functionality, aesthetics, accessories) before concluding the payment.
- **As-Is Basis**: Once both parties finalize the physical exchange and payment, the transaction is considered settled "As-Is". Subsequent claims regarding defects that were identifiable during physical inspection will not be accepted unless previously agreed in writing between Buyer and Seller.

## 3. Shipping & Delivery Transactions
For transactions conducted via courier delivery services:
- **Co-Inspection Right (Đồng kiểm)**: In accordance with Vietnamese consumer protection laws, Buyers are entitled to co-inspect the package exterior and basic contents upon delivery in the presence of the courier.
- **Reporting Window**: If an item arrives damaged, non-functioning, or materially different from the listing description, the Buyer must file an in-app dispute within **48 hours** from the timestamp of delivery.
- **Evidence Requirement**: The Buyer must provide unboxing videos, photos of shipping labels, and clear documentation of defects.

## 4. Eligible Conditions for Returns and Refunds
A refund or return request is deemed valid under the following circumstances:
- **Counterfeit / Fraudulent Items**: Item is proven to be fake or legally restricted despite being advertised as genuine.
- **Material Discrepancy**: Significant undisclosed defects (e.g., laptop motherboard failure, missing essential functional parts).
- **Non-Delivery**: Seller fails to deliver or dispatch the item after payment has been confirmed.
- **Wrong Item Delivered**: The delivered item does not match the model, specification, or listing ordered.

## 5. Ineligible Return Situations
Refunds will **not** be granted for:
- "Change of mind" requests for second-hand items.
- Minor wear-and-tear or cosmetic flaws clearly shown in listing photos or described in the seller's notes.
- Damage caused by misuse, unauthorized repair, or improper electrical usage by the Buyer after delivery.
- Requests submitted after the **48-hour** reporting window has lapsed.

## 6. Three-Step Dispute Resolution Process
In compliance with Article 36 of Decree No. 52/2013/ND-CP, Student Flea Market implements an impartial mediation mechanism:

1. **Step 1 - Direct Mutual Settlement (1 - 3 days)**: Buyer and Seller are encouraged to communicate via our in-app messaging to negotiate an amicable solution (e.g., partial refund, exchange, or return).
2. **Step 2 - Platform Mediation & Evidence Review (3 - 5 days)**: If direct settlement fails, either party may escalate the case to the Platform Administrator. Our compliance team will review chat logs, listing history, and video/photo evidence to issue a binding mediation decision.
3. **Step 3 - Judicial Escalation**: If either party refuses the Platform’s mediation outcome, the dispute may be submitted to the competent People's Committee, Consumer Protection Association, or People's Court in Vietnam. The Platform will cooperate with state authorities by providing transaction logs upon legal request.

## 7. Fraud Prevention & Account Sanctions
Users engaging in fraudulent listings, intentional delivery of damaged goods, or bad-faith return abuse will face:
- Immediate suspension of marketplace privileges.
- Permanent banning and blacklisting of student credentials.
- Referral to university disciplinary boards or law enforcement agencies if criminal fraud is substantiated.
