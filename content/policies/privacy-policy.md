# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026

## 1. Commiment to Data Protection
We value your policy and comply strictly with Decree No.13/2023/ND-CP on Personal Data Protection (PDPD) of Vietnam. This policy outlines how we collect, process, store and safeguard your personal data.

## 2. Types of Data Collected
- **Basic Personal Data**: Full name, email address, phone number, shipping address, date of birth.
- **Student Data**: University name, student ID number, enrollment status.
- **Technical & Usage Data**: IP address, device fingerprints, cookies, interaction timestamps.
- **Transaction Logs**: Order history, reviews, and customer support messages.

## 3. Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing
We only process data upon receiving your explicit consent for the following purposes:
- Creating and managing your user account.
- Facilitating direct buyer-seller communications and order management.
- Preventing fraud, abuse, and account takeovers.
- Complying with regulatory obligations under Vietnamese e-commerce statutes.
## 4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures
We do not sell personal data. Data may only be shared with:
- Payment gateway providers and SMS verification partners under strict data processing agreements.
- Cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., AWS Cloud Regions) with robust AES-256 encryption.
- Competent Vietnamese state authorities upon lawful statutory request.
## 5. Data Subject Rights (Article 9, Decree 13/2023/ND-CP)
As a Data Subject, you retain the right to:
- Be informed of your data processing activities.
- Give, withdraw, or limit your consent at any time.
- Request access to, correction of, or permanent erasure of your personal data.
- Object to data processing for marketing purposes.
